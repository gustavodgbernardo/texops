\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Writing a paper with several co-authors usually means exchanging files by e-mail or relying on a single online editor.
texops treats the paper as source code: every change goes through a pull request, is built in a reproducible container and is reviewed as a PDF.
The workflow builds on well-known tools such as \LaTeX{}~\cite{lamport1994latex} and latexdiff~\cite{latexdiff}.

The rest of this paper is organized as follows.
Section~\ref{sec:method} describes the method, Section~\ref{sec:results} presents the results and Section~\ref{sec:conclusion} concludes.
